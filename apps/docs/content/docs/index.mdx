OpenPost turns one idea into posts for the accounts you choose. You can draft, adapt, schedule, and review everything from one workspace.

## Start here

- [Quickstart](/guides/quickstart) gets your first post published.
- [Connect accounts](/guides/accounts) explains platform setup and permissions.
- [Create and publish](/guides/publishing) covers drafts, media, previews, and threads.
- [Self-host OpenPost](/self-hosting) is the operator guide.
- [API reference](/api-reference) is the generated HTTP contract.

## The usual flow

Choose a workspace, connect an account, write one publication, review each destination version, then publish now or schedule it. OpenPost keeps the publication and its destination renditions together so you can inspect what happened later.

## Other ways to work

Use the [automation guide](/guides/automation) for the CLI, MCP, and n8n. Use the [troubleshooting guide](/guides/troubleshooting) when a connection or delivery fails.
