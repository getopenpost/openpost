title: Schedule and calendar
description: Plan publication times and recover scheduled delivery.

---

Choose a date and time in the composer, check the displayed timezone, then select **Schedule**. The publication stays scheduled until OpenPost queues it for delivery. Open **Publications → Calendar** to see what is planned and open a post to inspect or edit it.

The Calendar has week and month views. If you are planning for another region, check its local time against the timezone shown in the composer before saving.

## Delivery states

The publication and each destination can be **Scheduled**, **Queued**, **Publishing**, **Published**, or **Failed**. One provider can publish successfully while another fails. Open the failed destination, fix the cause shown in its message, and retry it. Already published destinations do not need another delivery.

## Change a schedule

Open the scheduled publication, change its date or time, and save. In Calendar, you can also drag an item to preview another slot and drop it to save the new time. Open the post afterward to confirm the saved schedule.
