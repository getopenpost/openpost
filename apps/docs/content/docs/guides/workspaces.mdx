---
title: Workspaces and settings
description: Keep accounts, content, media, and access separated by workspace.
---

A workspace contains its publications, schedules, connected destinations, media, prompts, and members. Select the correct workspace before creating or reviewing work.

Open **Settings** to manage personal sign-in settings, workspace members and accounts, organization access, and instance settings when you administer a self-hosted deployment. Workspace roles control who can view, edit, and manage content.

Invite a teammate from workspace settings and assign the smallest role they need. Removing access stops future access to that workspace; it does not delete the workspace's publications.
