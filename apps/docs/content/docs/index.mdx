## Connect your accounts

Choose a workspace, then [connect the profiles, Pages, or channels](/guides/accounts) you want to publish to. If someone has invited you to a workspace, its connected accounts may already be ready to use.

On OpenPost Hosted, follow the connection steps in the app. On your own server, start with the [separate setup guide for your network](/self-hosting/integrations). Some need a developer app and approval before they can connect.

## Create and publish

A **publication** keeps your post, destinations, and schedule together. Each destination gets its own version, called a **rendition**. Start with shared text and media, then adjust each account's version before sending it.

- [Write a post or thread](/guides/publishing) and review each destination.
- [Choose a publishing time](/guides/scheduling) and plan in Calendar.
- [Upload and reuse media](/guides/media-library) across your workspace.
- [Make an image](/guides/image-editor), [edit a video](/guides/video-editor), or [trim a clip](/guides/quick-cut).
- [Record your screen and camera](/guides/recording) for a demo or update.

## Check what happened

Open a publication to see which destinations published and which need attention. A failed destination does not mean the others failed too.

Use [Analytics](/guides/analytics) to review the results your connected accounts provide, and [Inbox](/guides/inbox) to handle supported comments and messages. Availability depends on the provider and the permissions you granted.

If something went wrong, [troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting) helps you find the cause before retrying.

## Work with a team or automate a task

[Workspace roles](/guides/workspaces) control who can view and manage your content. Use separate workspaces when accounts, media, and team access should stay apart.

The [CLI and n8n guide](/guides/automation), [AI assistant guides](/mcp), and [API reference](/api-reference) cover other ways to work with the same accounts and publications. The API reference comes from OpenPost's API definition and includes request examples for every endpoint.

## Run your own instance

[Install with Docker Compose](/self-hosting), [configure your services](/self-hosting/configuration), and [plan backups and upgrades](/self-hosting/maintenance). The self-hosting section keeps server setup separate from the guides for everyday publishing.
