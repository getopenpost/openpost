A workspace keeps its posts, accounts, schedule, media, and members together. Choose the workspace from the switcher before you connect an account, write a post, or inspect results. The switcher marks the current workspace; if a post seems missing, check that selection first.

## Invite someone

Open **Workspace settings → Members**. As an admin, enter the teammate's email, choose a role, then select **Send invite**. The page shows a link you can copy after the invitation is created. Share it directly if email delivery is unavailable. The teammate must accept the invitation before they appear as a member.

Choose the role for what they need to do in this workspace:

| Role   | Access                                                      |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Admin  | Manage workspace access and settings                        |
| Editor | Create and manage workspace content                         |
| Client | View content, connect accounts, and decide assigned reviews |
| Viewer | View workspace content and settings                         |

Check **Invitations** to see whether the invitation is still waiting. An admin can resend or revoke it, and can change or remove a member's access later. Removing a member does not delete the workspace's posts.

## Find settings

Use **Workspace settings** for members and connected accounts. Your profile menu holds personal sign-in and notification preferences. If you manage a self-hosted deployment, instance settings are separate from any one workspace.

## Send a post to a client

Client review is optional unless an admin [requires it for every post](#require-client-review-for-every-post). Your usual draft and publishing flow stays the same until you explicitly send a post for review. The deployment must support client reviews, and the workspace must have an active client member.

1. Open a draft and finish its text, destination variants, media and provider settings.
2. Open the composer settings, select **Send to client**, choose the client, and add an optional note.
3. Check the submitted version and send it. The post now requires that client's approval before publishing.
4. The client opens the notification, reviews the submitted version, and chooses **Approve this version** or **Request changes**. Requesting changes requires feedback.
5. After approval, the author schedules or publishes the post. Approval itself never publishes it.

Editing submitted content requires another review. If the post was queued, the edit removes its pending delivery jobs and returns it to a draft. If you submitted a date, changing that date also requires another review. If you submitted no date, you can choose the first schedule after approval.

The review history keeps the submitted versions and decisions. Removing or changing the assigned client's role prevents further publishing under that approval. Automatic reposts of reviewed posts are disabled; create a separate post for a new placement. Inbox replies remain separate conversation actions.

### Require client review for every post

An admin can make client review mandatory for a workspace. Open **Workspace settings**, select **Require client review for every post** under **Client review**, and save.

While this is on, no post in the workspace can be scheduled, published, or retried until its assigned client approves the current version. This also applies to posts created through the API, the CLI, MCP, and Workflows. Posts that were already queued fail at delivery until they are approved, and automatic reposts are disabled. Add an active client member before turning it on, because posts cannot publish without one.

API and CLI tokens can turn the requirement on but not off. To turn it off, an admin must be signed in to OpenPost in a browser.
