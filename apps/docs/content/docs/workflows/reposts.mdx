---
title: Reposts and first comments
description: Manage existing repost rules and avoid duplicate follow-up replies.
icon: Workflow
---

Repost rules move into **Workflows** automatically. Their active or paused state, source and target accounts, engagement gates, repost cycles, and account grants stay intact. Scheduled reposts continue with their saved rules. There is no import button and no need to recreate them.

Open a repost rule to change its settings or inspect **Runs**, including custom reposts configured on individual posts. Expand a rule to see its accounts and timing; engagement gates remain collapsed until needed. Start with a one-day repost, a one-day and three-day cycle, or a repost after ten likes. New templates start paused so you can choose the accounts and enable them deliberately.

Pausing a rule stops new runs. Reposts already accepted continue. Per-post **Do not repost** and **Custom** choices still take precedence. First comments configured on a post remain separate. A follow-up workflow adds another reply, so avoid duplicating an existing first comment.
