title: Video Editor
description: Turn recordings and source clips into finished social videos.
icon: Clapperboard
---

Open [Video Editor](https://app.openpo.st/video-editor) in a current desktop version of Chrome or Edge. Editing and rendering run on your device. Exports have no watermark.

1. [Start a project](/video-editor/start) and add source files.
2. [Arrange and cut clips](/video-editor/timeline).
3. Adjust [color, motion, and effects](/video-editor/color-motion-and-effects), or add [captions and audio](/video-editor/transcript-and-audio).
4. [Export and attach the finished video](/video-editor/export-and-publish).

For cuts without changing the picture or audio mix, use [Quick Cut](/video-editor/quick-cut-and-recorder#trim-with-quick-cut). To capture your screen, camera, or microphone, use [Recorder](/video-editor/quick-cut-and-recorder#capture-with-recorder).

If a source, model, or export is unavailable, see [browser support and recovery](/video-editor/browser-and-recovery).

Use the built-in Assistant or [a connected MCP assistant](/mcp/mcp-guide/media#edit-an-open-project) for supported edits. You see changes live and can review or undo them in the editor. Keep the project open while it works.
