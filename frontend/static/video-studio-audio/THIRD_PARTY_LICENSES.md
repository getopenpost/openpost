# OpenPost bundled audio essentials

The eight mastered music loops and twelve mastered interface/transition sounds
exposed by OpenPost Video Studio were generated specifically for OpenPost by
`scripts/generate-video-studio-audio.mjs`. The checked-in WAV files are the
runtime assets; Video Studio does not synthesize them in the browser.

OpenPost dedicates these generated audio recordings to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They may be used, changed, and published without attribution.

`manifest.json` records each asset's author, license, duration, loop points,
mastering targets, byte size, and SHA-256 hash.
