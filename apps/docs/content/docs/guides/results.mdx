After publishing, open **Publications** and select the post. Each destination has its own status and provider result. A publication can have both successful and failed destinations; open a failed one to read its error before retrying it.

## See responses and performance

Open **Inbox** to read comments, replies, messages, and notifications if you enabled those features for the account. Open **Analytics** for account and post measurements. Turn on Analytics in account details first, then allow its first collection to finish. Providers return different measurements and update them at different times.

For eligible Bluesky and Mastodon accounts, **More → Find people** offers people to consider following. OpenPost follows someone only when you choose that action.

Disabling an optional account feature stops its future activity, but leaves its saved history available.
