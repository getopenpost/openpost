### Added

- Bluesky image posts and thread segments now support up to 10 JPEG, PNG, or WebP images. Posts with 1–4 images retain the existing image embed; posts with 5–10 images use the Bluesky gallery embed with ordered alt text and aspect ratios.
- A single animated GIF still publishes as a Bluesky video embed with GIF presentation. GIFs and videos must be the only attachment; OpenPost rejects a GIF mixed with images before uploading anything to Bluesky.
