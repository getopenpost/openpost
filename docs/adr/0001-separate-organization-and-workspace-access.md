# Separate Organization and Workspace access

An Organization is the billing, identity-policy, and administration boundary, while each Workspace is a separate content and collaboration boundary. Organization membership alone never grants Workspace content access; people enter through Workspace membership, and accepting a Workspace invitation may establish only the Organization affiliation required by shared billing and security rules. Each Organization has exactly one Owner, ownership transfer requires the recipient's acceptance, and OpenPost will retire the dormant direct Organization invitation model unless production inventory reveals data that needs a different disposition. The final Workspace cannot be deleted through the ordinary Workspace flow because removing it requires an explicit Organization lifecycle that resolves billing and pending external work. This keeps routine collaboration scoped to the resource being shared and prevents Organization affiliation from silently exposing every Workspace.
