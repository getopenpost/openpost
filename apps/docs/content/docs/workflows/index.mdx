Open **More → Workflows** in your workspace. Workflows uses your connected accounts and workspace access.

[Build your first workflow](/workflows/first-workflow) to create a release announcement that waits for review.

## Build and run

- [Build steps on the canvas](/workflows/editor) and [Pass step outputs with variables](/workflows/variables).
- [Choose a trigger](/workflows/triggers), [Generate text and decide branches with AI](/workflows/ai), or [Call APIs with saved connections](/workflows/connections).
- [Test previews, nodes, and live runs](/workflows/testing) before activating automatic runs.
- [Review and schedule posts](/workflows/review-and-schedule), then [Inspect and stop runs](/workflows/runs).

## Examples

- [Prepare a weekly feed digest](/workflows/examples/feed-digest).
- [Share only relevant releases](/workflows/examples/relevant-releases).
