# Todoist sample material

The launch film follows an invented Todoist 12 release, so it shows a real brand's real look. The update is sample material and OpenPost is not affiliated with Todoist.

| File                                      | Source                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `mark.png`                                | Todoist app icon, supplied by the maintainer on 2026-10-30                                     |
| `logo.svg`                                | Header logo on https://www.todoist.com/, read on 2026-10-31 with its CSS colours resolved      |
| `og.png`                                  | https://www.todoist.com/static/ogimages/en/og-image-home.png, read on 2026-10-31               |
| `today.png`                               | Todoist marketing screenshots, supplied by the maintainer on 2026-10-30                        |
| `voice.png`, `quick-add.png`, `board.png` | Todoist marketing artwork, supplied by the maintainer on 2026-10-30                            |
| `Inter.ttf`                               | Inter, the body font on todoist.com, from google/fonts under the SIL OFL (`LICENSE-Inter.txt`) |
| `ShantellSans.ttf`                        | Shantell Sans, its handwritten font, from google/fonts under the SIL OFL                       |

Todoist's headings use Graphik and its serif is Caecilia. Both are commercial fonts, so the film sets headings in Inter
instead of copying them from Todoist's CDN. Todoist owns its name, logos and artwork. They are here for the sample
scenes only and are not relicensed by this repository.
