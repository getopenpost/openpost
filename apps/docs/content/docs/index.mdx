## Keep your work moving

Bring your accounts, drafts, and media into one workspace. Write a publication, adjust its version for each destination, then publish now or choose a time. Check the result for each account without losing the original idea.

- [Prepare images and video](/guides/media), from reusable media to finished exports.
- [Review results and replies](/guides/results) after your posts go live.
- [Share a workspace](/guides/workspaces) and give each person the access they need.
- [Fix a connection or failed post](/guides/troubleshooting) and pick up where you left off.

## Connect your own tools

Use the [CLI, an AI assistant, or n8n](/guides/automation) to work with OpenPost. The [API reference](/api-reference) includes authentication, request examples, and every endpoint, generated from the same contract as the app.

## Run it yourself

The [self-hosting guide](/self-hosting) covers a Docker installation, configuration, backups, and upgrades. If you use [OpenPost Hosted](https://app.openpo.st), your server is already taken care of.
