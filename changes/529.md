### Fixed

- Instagram carousel posts no longer stay pending for up to 24 hours after Meta times out downloading one of their items. OpenPost retries the unfinished step.
- When Meta rate-limits an account, OpenPost waits at least 15 minutes, or Meta's own estimate of up to an hour, before checking a pending Instagram post again. It used to check every 10 seconds, which kept the account rate-limited. Pending posts are also checked less often the longer they stay pending.
