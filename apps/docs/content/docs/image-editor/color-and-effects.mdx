---
title: Color and effects
description: Crop images, adjust a layer or page, add effects, and remove backgrounds.
---

Select an image layer to crop or adjust it. Open **Color** to grade a layer or the whole page.

## Crop or correct an image

In **Properties → Crop**, choose the visible part of the source. The original file stays unchanged.

Choose **Warm**, **Cool**, or **Mono** for a preset look, or change tone and color controls yourself. Compare the result at the size you will export.

## Grade a layer or page

1. Open **Color**.
2. Choose **Layer** for selected unlocked images, or **Pages** for the combined page output.
3. Apply a preset or adjust tone, wheels, or curves. Curves support the master, red, green, and blue channels.
4. Compare **Before** and **After**. Reset a section or the whole grade if needed.

Scopes update while you work so you can inspect light and color levels.

## Add layer effects

Use **Properties** to add blend modes, strokes, shadows, or masks. Available effects depend on the layer type. A mask changes a layer's visible shape without changing its source.

Save an effect combination as a [workspace preset](/image-editor/templates-and-brand#save-layer-effect-presets). Check text and logo edges at the final output size.

## Remove a background

Select an image and choose **Remove background**. Processing runs in your browser and does not send the image to a background-removal service.

The result is a transparent PNG in Media. The original stays unchanged, and you can undo the layer change. Large images may use a smaller temporary copy for processing.
