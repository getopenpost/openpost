title: Schedule and calendar
description: Plan publication times and recover scheduled delivery.

---

Set a date and time in the composer, then choose **Schedule**. Open **Calendar** to review scheduled publications by day, week, or month. Open a publication from the calendar to edit or inspect it.

The composer shows the timezone used for your schedule. Check the timezone before scheduling work for another region.

## Delivery states

The publication and each rendition can be scheduled, queued, publishing, published, or failed. A provider can accept one rendition while another fails. Open the failed rendition for its provider message and retry when the cause is fixed.

## Change a schedule

Open the publication, change its date or time, and save. Drag a calendar item to preview a new time, then drop it to save.
